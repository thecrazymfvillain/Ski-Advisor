Home Ski Tuning Kit: What to Buy First

You can start with five tools and add the rest as you go. Here's the order to buy them in.

Some links on this page may earn us a commission. It doesn't change what we recommend.

Start here: waxing

  • Waxing iron: a dedicated ski iron with a steady temperature. Shop
  • Universal wax: works across temperatures while you learn. Shop
  • Plexi scraper: removes excess wax without hurting the base. Shop
  • Nylon brush: opens the base structure after scraping. Shop
  • Ski straps or a vise: hold the ski steady, base-up. Shop

Next: edges

  • Side edge guide: at your angle, usually 2° (88°). Shop
  • Diamond stones: 200, 400 and 600 grit. Shop
  • Gummy stone: for deburring and detuning. Shop

Later: the extras

  • Temperature-specific waxes once you know your conditions. See the wax chart. Shop
  • Brass and horsehair brushes for cold and warm wax. Shop
  • Base edge guide for deburring the base edge. Shop
  • P-tex candles for small base repairs. Shop
  • A polishing pad for the final buff. Shop
