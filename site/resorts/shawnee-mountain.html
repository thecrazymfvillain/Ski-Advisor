        Skiing Shawnee Mountain's snow

        Shawnee Mountain is a ski area in Smithfield, Pennsylvania with 10 lifts and 27 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 480 ft (145 m) to 1,150 ft (349 m), 670 ft of vertical.

        Frequent freeze-thaw cycles and a lot of machine-made snow make firm, icy conditions common, especially in the morning. The mapped runs mix beginner, intermediate and advanced terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 810 ft (247 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Shawnee Mountain

        • Edges matter more than wax on firm snow. Sharpen often; many skiers here run a 2° or 3° side edge.
        • Machine-made snow is abrasive. Use a harder, colder wax than the temperature alone suggests.
        • With 670 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
