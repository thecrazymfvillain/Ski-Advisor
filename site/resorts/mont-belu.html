        Skiing Mont-Bélu's snow

        Mont-Bélu is a ski area in Saguenay, Quebec, Canada with 2 lifts and 15 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 70 ft (22 m) to 630 ft (193 m), 560 ft of vertical.

        Frequent freeze-thaw cycles and a lot of machine-made snow make firm, icy conditions common, especially in the morning. A large share of the mapped runs are advanced or expert terrain, where sharp edges pay off.

        The forecast on this page is for 350 ft (107 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Mont-Bélu

        • Edges matter more than wax on firm snow. Sharpen often; many skiers here run a 2° or 3° side edge.
        • Machine-made snow is abrasive. Use a harder, colder wax than the temperature alone suggests.
        • With 560 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
