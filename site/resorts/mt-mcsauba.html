        Skiing Mt. McSauba's snow

        Mt. McSauba is a ski area in Charlevoix, Michigan with 4 lifts and 7 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 600 ft (184 m) to 750 ft (229 m), 140 ft of vertical.

        Cold winters, modest natural snowfall and a lot of snowmaking mean firm, dry, abrasive snow on most days. Most of the mapped runs are beginner and intermediate terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 680 ft (206 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Mt. McSauba

        • Machine-made snow wears wax quickly. Re-wax every few ski days, and pick the colder wax when you're unsure.
        • Short runs on firm snow reward sharp edges. A quick polish with a fine stone before each day keeps them biting.
        • With 140 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
