About The Ski Advisor

Wax and tuning advice for the snow you'll actually ski.

What the site does

Pick any of 745 ski areas in the US, Canada and Mexico and you get the wax color and iron temperature for today, tomorrow or this weekend, edge settings for how you ski, and a step-by-step home tune. The guides cover the rest, from a first wax job to race day.

How the recommendations work

  • Forecast where you ski. Every resort's forecast is for its mid-mountain elevation, not the base, refreshed every few hours.
  • Snow, not air. We estimate the snow temperature from the air temperature and sky cover, and the surface type: new, packed, refrozen, man-made or wet.
  • Wax from the snow. The wax color comes from the snow temperature, one step colder for sharp new snow or abrasive man-made snow, and warm with extra brushing on wet days.
  • Edges from how you ski. Edge angles are a one-time setup for your style of skiing. The snow only changes how much edge work is worth doing.

Where the data comes from

Weather comes from MET Norway under the CC BY 4.0 license. Resort locations, elevations, lifts and runs come from OpenStreetMap contributors and OpenSkiMap under the Open Database License. Volunteers maintain that map, so some details will be wrong. If you spot one, tell us or fix it in OpenStreetMap; the fix reaches this site at the next data refresh.

Starting points, not rules

The temperature on your wax label beats our chart, and a snow thermometer beats any forecast. Use the recommendations as a starting point and adjust from what you see on the hill.

How the site makes money

As an Amazon Associate, The Ski Advisor earns from qualifying purchases. When you buy through a Shop link, Amazon pays us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Commissions don't change what we recommend. We're independent and not affiliated with any resort or wax brand.
