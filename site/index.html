    How it works

    1. Forecast at mid-mountainWe pull temperature, humidity and new snow at the elevation you'll actually ski, not at the base.
    2. Snow, not airWe estimate the snow temperature and surface: new, packed, refrozen, man-made or wet.
    3. Wax, edges, stepsYou get the wax color and iron setting for the snow, edge settings for how you ski or ride, and a tune walkthrough set to match.
