        Skiing Sundown Mountain Resort's snow

        Sundown Mountain Resort is a ski area in Asbury, Iowa with 6 lifts and 16 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 720 ft (220 m) to 1,150 ft (351 m), 430 ft of vertical.

        Cold winters, modest natural snowfall and a lot of snowmaking mean firm, dry, abrasive snow on most days. Most of the mapped runs are beginner and intermediate terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 940 ft (286 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Sundown Mountain Resort

        • Machine-made snow wears wax quickly. Re-wax every few ski days, and pick the colder wax when you're unsure.
        • Short runs on firm snow reward sharp edges. A quick polish with a fine stone before each day keeps them biting.
        • With 430 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
