Privacy policy

What happens to information when you use The Ski Advisor.

Effective November 19, 2026

The Ski Advisor (theskiadvisor.com) is a free website. You don't need an account, and we don't sell personal information. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the site.

Information you give us

If you use the contact form, we receive your email address, your message, the topic you chose and, if you give it, your name. We use it only to reply to you. The form is handled by our host, Netlify, which stores submissions for us. We delete messages when we no longer need them, or sooner if you ask.

Information stored in your browser

The site remembers a few choices in your own browser's local storage: your temperature units, how you ski or ride, training or race-day mode, the mountains you save to My mountains, and which tuning steps you've checked off. This stays on your device; it isn't sent to us. Clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Your location

If you press Near me, your browser asks for permission to share your location. If you allow it, the site uses it only inside your browser to sort resorts by distance. Your location isn't sent to us or stored.

Services that see your visit

  • Netlify hosts the site. Like any web host, it processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. See Netlify's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts provides the site's fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) provides the 3D graphics library for the home page flyover. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Weather comes from MET Norway, but our server requests it; your browser doesn't contact MET Norway and we don't send them anything about you.

Amazon links

As an Amazon Associate, The Ski Advisor earns from qualifying purchases. When you click a Shop link, you go to Amazon, and Amazon may use cookies to record that you came from this site so it can pay us a commission. What Amazon collects is covered by Amazon's privacy notice.

Analytics and advertising

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful. It doesn't use cookies or track you across sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies; if that changes, we'll update this page first.

Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you from the contact form, and ask us to correct or delete it, by contacting us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and its effective date.
