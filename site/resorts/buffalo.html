        Skiing Buffalo Ski Club's snow

        Buffalo Ski Club is a ski area in Boston, New York with 6 lifts and 35 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 1,010 ft (308 m) to 1,480 ft (452 m), 470 ft of vertical.

        Frequent freeze-thaw cycles and a lot of machine-made snow make firm, icy conditions common, especially in the morning. The mapped runs mix beginner, intermediate and advanced terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 1,250 ft (380 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Buffalo Ski Club

        • Edges matter more than wax on firm snow. Sharpen often; many skiers here run a 2° or 3° side edge.
        • Machine-made snow is abrasive. Use a harder, colder wax than the temperature alone suggests.
        • With 470 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
