Home Ski Tuning Kit: What to Buy First

You can start with five tools and add the rest as you go. Here's the order to buy them in.

Start here: waxing

  • Universal wax: works across temperatures while you learn. Shop on Amazon
  • Plexi scraper: removes excess wax without hurting the base. Shop on Amazon

  • Brass and horsehair brushes for cold and warm wax. Shop on Amazon

  • P-tex candles for small base repairs. Shop on Amazon
  • A polishing pad for the final buff. Shop on Amazon

What not to buy

  • A clothes iron. It runs too hot and uneven, and steam holes leave stripes in the wax.
  • A metal scraper for wax. It gouges the base. Plexi is for wax; steel scrapers are for P-tex repairs.
  • Fixed-angle pocket sharpeners for regular use. They're handy for a quick touch-up but can't match a proper guide's consistency.
  • A huge kit up front. Start with the basics and add tools as you need them.

Snowboard kits

The same tools work for snowboards. A wider vise or padded bench supports help, and you'll go through more wax, because a board's base is much wider than a ski's. See how to wax a snowboard.

Taking care of your tools

Keep a separate brush for each type of wax if you can, rinse diamond stones after use and let them dry, and resharpen the plexi scraper's edge when it stops cutting cleanly. Store the iron with its base clean, so old wax doesn't scorch the next time you use it.
