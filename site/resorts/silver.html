        Skiing Silver Mountain Resort's snow

        Silver Mountain Resort is a ski area in Kellogg, Idaho with 7 lifts and 79 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 4,170 ft (1,272 m) to 6,250 ft (1,904 m), 2,070 ft of vertical.

        It sits far enough inland that storms drop lighter, drier snow, with cold nights and milder, sunny afternoons between storms. The mapped runs mix beginner, intermediate and advanced terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 5,210 ft (1,588 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Silver Mountain Resort

        • After a storm, dry new snow calls for a colder wax than the temperature alone suggests.
        • Clear nights make first-chair snow colder than the air. Plan for the colder end of the wax range in the morning.
        • The mid-mountain forecast here, at 5,210 ft, covers most of the terrain.
