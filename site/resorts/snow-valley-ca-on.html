        Skiing Snow Valley Ski Resort's snow

        Snow Valley Ski Resort is a ski area in Springwater, Ontario, Canada with 8 lifts and 44 mapped runs. Its lifts reach from about 770 ft (236 m) to 1,060 ft (323 m), 290 ft of vertical.

        Frequent freeze-thaw cycles and a lot of machine-made snow make firm, icy conditions common, especially in the morning. The mapped runs mix beginner, intermediate and advanced terrain.

        The forecast on this page is for 920 ft (279 m), halfway up the mountain.

        Tuning tips for Snow Valley Ski Resort

        • Edges matter more than wax on firm snow. Sharpen often; many skiers here run a 2° or 3° side edge.
        • Machine-made snow is abrasive. Use a harder, colder wax than the temperature alone suggests.
        • With 290 ft of vertical, conditions are similar top to bottom, so this forecast covers the whole hill.
